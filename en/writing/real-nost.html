Reality

October 8, 2026

Translated from Russian.

If it's honest, it's valuable.

I turn 36 today. Today is my birthday.

I built Dok. It's a Telegram bot that knows motivational interviewing, solution-focused therapy, ACT, and the Rogerian approach. For couples – the Gottman method, attachment theory, nonviolent communication.

You can talk to it on your own. Or you can add it to a group chat with your partner, and it will work with the two of you.

How many experts would it take to build something like this out of people? How much money?

I want psychological support to be accessible: any hour, no appointment, no waiting list. And I want it to work.

I don't want to replace the psychologist. I want to bring things together. Maybe you see a specialist once a week, and between sessions Dok is there.

I add new techniques fast. But whether Dok reaches a wide audience depends on quality. And I can only learn about quality from the first users.

I really need first users.

If you see a psychologist, a coach, or go to group therapy – try Dok. Talk to it and tell me how it went. I'll read every message.

What I think about Dok isn't reality. Reality is how it works for you.

Your feedback is the best birthday present I could get.
